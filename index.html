Computational materials science · Energy storage

Dr Alok Kumar Tripathi

Assistant Professor of Physics, Ramjas College, University of Delhi

I study how ions move through battery electrolytes, using molecular dynamics and density-functional theory to connect local coordination chemistry to transport. The aim is electrolyte design rules for safer lithium and sodium batteries and supercapacitors.

    Research

    What I work on

    Polymer electrolytes

    How polymer chain length, plasticizers and salt choice control cation solvation and diffusion in PAN, PTMC and related hosts.

    Fillers and interfaces

    Functional inorganic fillers that trap anions and raise the share of current carried by the cation.

    Solid ion conductors

    Doping and defect arrangement in anti-perovskite lithium conductors, from first principles.

    Methods: classical MD (OPLS4) · DFT (Quantum ESPRESSO) · RDF, residence-time and MSD analysis

    Open to collaboration. Experimental groups working on polymer or solid electrolytes are welcome to get in touch for joint simulation studies.
